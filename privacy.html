Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-16

This Privacy Policy explains how pineapple_mo ("we", "us", "our"), the operator of the TikTok account @pineapple_mo, collects, uses, and protects information when using TikTok's Content Posting API and related services.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize our application to connect with your TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit / Content Posting API, we may access and store:

  • Basic TikTok profile information (username, display name, avatar)
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens issued by TikTok
  • Video content, captions, and metadata that you submit for publishing through our tool

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your TikTok account and publish content on your behalf, only as directed by you
  • To manage and schedule content uploads to the connected TikTok account
  • To troubleshoot and improve the reliability of our publishing workflow

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or TikTok data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Access tokens and content data are stored securely and used only for the purpose of operating the TikTok account listed above. We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access.

4. Data Retention

We retain TikTok authorization tokens only as long as necessary to provide the publishing service. You may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings, which will immediately invalidate our access.

5. Third-Party Services

Our service integrates with the TikTok Content Posting API. Your use of TikTok itself remains subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data we hold about you by contacting us using the information below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via TikTok @pineapple_mo.
